# 🎬 Echoes — Demo Video Script

A line-by-line screen-recording script. Read the **SAY** lines as voiceover while
you perform the **DO** actions on screen. Every feature of Echoes appears at least
once; the checklist at the bottom tracks them all.

---

## What Echoes is (say this in your own words if you like)

We save photos, videos, and chat logs — but none of them preserve the *feeling* of
a moment, or the **sound** of the people in it. When the people in our memories are
gone, so is the sound of them.

**Echoes** turns a written or spoken memory into a short, emotionally narrated,
cinematic story — voiced aloud by **Murf**, scored with an emotion-tuned ambient
bed, and — in its signature mode — performed as a **conversation between two people
across time, in two distinct voices.**

> *"Photos preserve how moments looked. Echoes preserves how they felt."*

---

## The memory we'll use

Paste this into **Create → Write memory**:

> Every summer evening, my grandfather and I shelled peas on the back porch while
> his old radio played songs older than both of us. His hands were rough from the
> fields but gentle, and he told the same stories as if each one were brand new.
> The last summer — before his memory started to slip — he squeezed my hand and
> said, "Promise me you won't forget the songs." I never have.

**Dual-Voice config:** Mode = *Dual voice* · Preset = *You & Grandfather* ·
Speaker A = **Me** (Documentary) · Speaker B = **Grandfather** (Podcast).

---

## Before you hit record

- **Warm the server.** On the free tier the site sleeps after ~15 min idle. Open
  the URL ~60 seconds before recording so it isn't cold-starting on camera.
- **Generation takes ~15–30s** (AI + Murf + audio mixing). Either let it run and
  **trim the wait in editing**, or **pre-create both the solo and duet stories
  first** and simply *open* them live for a seamless take.
- Record at 1080p, hide bookmarks/extensions, and use a clean browser profile.
- Let the audio **breathe** — the silences after narration are the emotional beats.

## SCENE 0 — Cold open · Landing page `/`

**DO:** Land on the homepage. Slowly scroll: hero → "The Premise" → the film-reel
"Process" → the scene-still cards.

**SAY:**
- "We save photos. We save videos. We save every chat we've ever sent."
- "But none of them keep the one thing that actually fades — the *sound* of the people in them."
- "This is Echoes. It turns a memory into a narrated, cinematic story — in a real voice."
- *(pause on the hero)* "Photos preserve how a moment looked. Echoes preserves how it *felt*."

## SCENE 1 — Create a memory · `/create`

**DO:** Click **Create**. Point at the green service-health pills (Murf / Gemini / Transcription).

**SAY:**
- "Everything's live — Murf for the voice, Google Gemini for the storytelling."
- "I'll start with a memory about my grandfather."

**DO:** Click the **Voice note** tab to reveal the upload box, then switch back to **Write memory** and paste the memory text.

**SAY:**
- "I can type a memory… or just *speak* it — Echoes transcribes a voice note for me."
- "Here's the moment I want to keep."

**DO:** Click **Add a memory photo** and choose an old photo. Then click **Detect emotion**.

**SAY:**
- "I'll add an old photograph — it becomes a slow Ken-Burns backdrop during playback."
- "And I'll let the AI read the emotional tone… *nostalgic*. Exactly right."

**DO:** Click **Preview story** and let the enhanced narration appear.

**SAY:**
- "Before I commit, I can preview how the AI will reimagine my rough notes as cinematic narration."

**DO:** Choose **Story style** = *Netflix Documentary*; show the **Language** chips; show the **Narrator persona** grid.

**SAY:**
- "Seven storytelling styles — documentary, Pixar, a letter to your younger self, bedtime, motivational…"
- "Five languages, each with a native Murf voice… and a choice of narrators."
- "I'll keep this one a documentary, in English."

**DO:** Click **Transform into cinematic story**.

**SAY:**
- "And now the memory becomes a story."

## SCENE 2 — Cinematic playback · `/story/[id]`

- "That's a Murf voice — mixed with an emotion-tuned ambient score underneath."

**SAY:**

## SCENE 8 — ✦ The signature: Dual-Voice Legacy Mode
